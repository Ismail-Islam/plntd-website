Delete Your Account

PLNTD loyalty app

This page explains how to delete your PLNTD account and the data associated with it. PLNTD is operated by PLNTD LONDON LTD.

Delete your account in the app

The quickest way is directly in the PLNTD app:

  1. Open the PLNTD app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon on the Home screen).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are permanently removed straight away.

Or request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account at admin@plntdco.com with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile (name and email address)
  • Your points balance and full transaction history
  • Your in-app message read history

What may be kept

We may retain a limited amount of information where we are required to by law (for example, basic records needed for accounting or fraud-prevention). Any such data is kept only for as long as the law requires and is then deleted.
