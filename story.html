Why we exist

The place we wished existed.

PLNTD Co. started from a simple frustration: there was nowhere local to enjoy genuinely fresh, healthy food and drink made with quality ingredients.

Having always been passionate about nutrition, wellbeing, and a more balanced way of living, I wanted to create the kind of place I felt was missing from our community. Somewhere people could stop for a freshly pressed juice, a nourishing smoothie, a speciality coffee, or a great lunch and know that quality came first.

We're proud to be a small independent business. We believe in supporting local suppliers wherever possible, creating opportunities within our community, and serving products we'd happily enjoy ourselves every day.

More than anything, we wanted PLNTD Co. to feel welcoming. A place where people can meet, relax, work, catch up with friends, or simply make a better choice for their day.

Because eating well shouldn't be complicated. It should be accessible, enjoyable, and part of everyday life.

What makes us different

Quality is in everything we do.

At PLNTD Co. quality isn't reserved for one part of the menu — it's the standard behind everything we serve.

From speciality coffee and freshly pressed juices to made-to-order smoothies, premium sandwiches, and baked goods, every product is chosen with care and made using ingredients we're proud to put our name behind.

We believe people can taste the difference when ingredients are better, when food is prepared properly, and when a business genuinely cares about the details.

That's why we work with trusted suppliers, source premium ingredients, and never compromise on the standards that matter most.

Because when you're serving your own community, cutting corners simply isn't an option.

Fresh every day

Real ingredients. Prepared fresh.

Freshness sits at the heart of PLNTD Co.

Our juices are pressed fresh, our smoothies are blended to order, and every item on our menu is selected with the same focus on quality, flavour, and nutrition.

Whether you're grabbing a morning coffee, a post-gym smoothie, a healthy lunch, or something to enjoy on the go, our goal is simple: serve food and drink that helps people feel good.

No unnecessary shortcuts. No compromise on ingredients.

Just great products, made with care, for people who value quality as much as we do.
