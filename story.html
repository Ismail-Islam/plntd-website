Est. 2026 · Leyton, London

Good things
grow here.

PLNTD started with a blender, a market stall, and a stubbornly simple idea — that a cold-pressed juice should taste like the fruit it's made from, and nothing else.

"We were tired of paying £8 for something that was 40% apple juice from concentrate. So we quit our jobs and made something better."
— Kwame & Isla, Co-founders
How it started

A market stall in Hackney.

In the summer of 2026, Kwame Asante and Isla Reid turned up to Broadway Market with a cold-press juicer, a borrowed gazebo, and 40 kilos of produce from New Spitalfields. They sold out in two hours.

Word spread. Within three months they were at three markets a week. By spring 2023 they'd signed a lease on 144 High Road Leyton — a former fishmonger's they spent four months converting themselves, tile by tile.

The mission

Real food.
No shortcuts.

Every juice is pressed on-site each morning. Every smoothie is blended to order. There's no pre-made, no concentrate, no sitting in a chilled bag since Thursday. If a fruit isn't in season, it's not on the menu.

The coffee comes from a small roaster in Bermondsey who sources single-origin beans direct from cooperatives in Ethiopia and Colombia. Oat milk as standard, no surcharge.

Community

Built for the neighbourhood.

Leyton isn't Shoreditch. We're not trying to be a concept store or a lifestyle brand. We're a corner bar where you grab a green juice on the way to work, or sit in with a flat white on a Saturday morning.

We partner with local schools on nutrition workshops, source produce from two East London growers, and run a pay-it-forward wall where customers can leave a drink for someone who needs it.

What we stand for
01

Real ingredients

If we can't name where it came from, it's not going in the cup. No fillers, no flavourings, no concentrates.

02

Made fresh

Everything is pressed or blended the same day it's served. We close early when we run out — and we're fine with that.

03

Low waste

Pulp goes to a local bakery for bread. Packaging is compostable. We track our food waste weekly and publish it monthly.

04

Fair prices

A cold-pressed juice shouldn't cost more than a pint. We keep margins honest and pass the savings on.
